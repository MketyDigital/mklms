## Enterprise sales copy

### Infrastructure that fits the customer, not the other way around

Enterprise accounts are not a fixed oversized package. Mkety can provision the exact commercial and technical profile agreed with the customer: price, storage, delivery, request volume, team seats, file-size limits, billing term and infrastructure mode.

A customer may have Enterprise capabilities with Starter-sized limits, or a much larger dedicated arrangement. Enterprise is about control and requirements—not simply buying the biggest public plan.

Enterprise options can include:
- exact private pricing;
- exact capacity limits;
- custom billing terms;
- branded media hostname;
- regional or dedicated infrastructure eligibility;
- retention/deletion-protection requirements;
- assisted migration/export;
- written SLA terms where agreed;
- dedicated onboarding and operator support.

## Serious-customer FAQ

### Where are my files stored?
Mkety Media uses managed enterprise-grade object-storage infrastructure selected by service tier. Provider identities are an internal infrastructure decision unless a customer contract specifically requires disclosure or a named provider.

### Is storage redundant?
Yes. The standard storage layer uses infrastructure designed for eleven-nines annual durability with multiple redundant copies/erasure protection across storage hardware and data centres within a geographic region.

### Are there automatic backups?
Standard plans include infrastructure redundancy but do not include a separate point-in-time backup product. Enterprise customers can request retention/deletion protection, separate backup policy or managed migration/export requirements.

### What uptime is guaranteed?
The standard storage layer is backed by infrastructure with a 99.9% availability SLA. Mkety only provides a separate contractual customer SLA where that commitment is included in the Enterprise order.

### Can I leave Mkety with all my files?
Yes. Customers can export a complete JSON/CSV library manifest and generate download-all scripts for macOS/Linux or Windows. Enterprise customers may request an assisted migration.

### Can I use my own domain?
Yes for enabled custom/Enterprise accounts. A customer can request a hostname such as `media.example.com`; Mkety provisions TLS/routing and provides the DNS record to add.

### Will Mkety charge unlimited overage?
No. Public plans use prepaid hard caps. Customers can upgrade or buy prepaid extra capacity. Mkety does not intentionally create unbounded post-paid usage debt.

### Can Mkety support data residency or dedicated infrastructure?
These are Enterprise requirements and depend on the agreed infrastructure pool, geography and commercial terms.

### Who owns uploaded content?
The customer retains ownership of uploaded content. Mkety processes, stores and delivers the content to provide the service.

### Are files encrypted?
Traffic uses HTTPS/TLS and storage infrastructure encrypts stored data. Provider/storage credentials are not exposed to customers.

### What happens when I delete a file?
Mkety removes the source object and requests cache invalidation. Standard delivery uses bounded edge caching. Formal retention, legal hold or deletion-protection requirements should be specified in an Enterprise policy.

Before quoting Enterprise, ask:
1. Approximate total storage now and expected growth?
2. Monthly delivery/egress volume?
3. Approximate request volume?
4. Largest individual file?
5. Images only, video, downloads, or mixed?
6. Required team seats?
7. Need a branded custom domain?
8. Need geographic placement/data residency?
9. Need retention, deletion protection or separate backup?
10. Need a contractual uptime/SLA schedule?
11. Need assisted migration from another provider?
12. Monthly, quarterly, half-year or annual commitment?

## Positioning rule

Do not publicly name underlying storage providers as the product. Sell **Mkety Media**. Provider-specific details remain internal infrastructure information unless disclosure is required by an Enterprise agreement, compliance requirement or customer architecture review.
