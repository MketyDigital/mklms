## Product page — Enterprise

# Enterprise does not mean “buy the biggest plan”

Mkety Media Enterprise is for customers who need terms that do not fit a public package.

A customer can have Enterprise capabilities with small limits and a private price, or a much larger regional/dedicated arrangement.

Possible Enterprise requirements:
- exact private pricing;
- custom storage/delivery/request limits;
- custom file-size limits;
- additional team seats;
- branded media domain;
- assisted migration;
- retention/deletion protection requirements;
- data-residency requirements;
- regional or dedicated infrastructure;
- private/signed delivery requirements;
- contractual SLA schedule where agreed.

## FAQ snippets for Mkety.com

**Can I use Mkety Media outside Mkety LMS?**  
Yes. Mkety Media is a standalone Mkety product and its file links can be used on websites, apps, landing pages and other systems.

**Can I host video?**  
Yes. Mkety Media supports images, video and general file uploads. Large files use multipart uploads and delivery supports byte-range requests.

**Can I use my own media domain?**  
Custom/Enterprise accounts can request a branded hostname such as `media.example.com`.

**Can I export everything later?**  
Yes. Full-library manifests and download-all scripts are available from the customer dashboard.

**Are files private?**  
Standard delivery URLs are public to anyone who has the URL. Private/signed access is a custom/Enterprise requirement.

**Do you charge unlimited overage?**  
No. Public plans use prepaid hard caps; customers upgrade or buy extra capacity.
