High-Performance Free Video Storage Guide

To guarantee lightning-fast load times and perfect playback on all devices (especially iOS/Android mobile), use one of these highly-compatible storage options:

Option 1: Dropbox (Recommended Free) Paste any standard Dropbox link. Our backend automatically proxies it to dl.dropboxusercontent.com for instant, lag-free direct CDN buffering.
Option 2: YouTube (Highly Recommended) Paste any unlisted or public YouTube video. Our system automatically handles it via our white-labeled player layer, keeping chats synchronized perfectly!
Option 3: Bunny.net / Cloudflare Stream (Premium) Extremely cheap paid solutions (fraction of a cent). Provides high-speed direct global CDNs and prevents any lag or browser security blocks.
Option 4: Google Drive (Limits Apply) Google Drive files are supported but Google limits high-speed direct downloads to files under 100MB. Larger files will fail to play directly. Keep sizes small or use Dropbox!
